## About The Company

The Glass Doctor is a Sharjah-based glass and architectural solutions company serving clients across the UAE. We specialize in transforming glass and aluminum into sophisticated, functional, and durable solutions for residential, commercial, and architectural projects.

From precision glass tempering, bending, lamination, polishing and mitering to partitions, smart glass, frosting, sandblasting, back painting and acid etching, our expertise combines advanced technology with meticulous craftsmanship.

We also deliver complete architectural solutions, including aluminum windows & doors, curtain walls, skylights, and pergolas, designed to complement modern spaces with elegance and performance.

**Mission Statement:**  
"At The Glass Doctor, we believe every project deserves precision, quality, and attention to detail. With a commitment to reliable service and exceptional finishing, we turn ideas into glass and architectural solutions that stand out."

## ⚙️ Our Process (5-Step Workflow)

### Step 1: Consultation
Understanding your project requirements, site conditions, and design vision.
- **Duration:** 1-2 days
- **Deliverable:** Initial assessment and recommendations

### Step 2: Design & Selection
Selecting the right glass products, finishes, and technical specifications for your project.
- **Duration:** 3-5 days
- **Deliverable:** Detailed proposal with options and pricing

### Step 3: Measurement
Precise on-site measurements and technical documentation for accurate fabrication.
- **Duration:** 1 day
- **Deliverable:** Technical drawings and measurements

### Step 4: Fabrication
Expert manufacturing using advanced technology to exact specifications.
- **Duration:** 7-14 days (depending on scope)
- **Deliverable:** Quality-tested glass products ready for installation

### Step 5: Installation & Support
Professional installation by skilled technicians with post-installation support.
- **Duration:** 2-7 days (depending on scope)
- **Deliverable:** Completed installation with warranty
