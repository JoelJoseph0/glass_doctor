## 📸 Image Guidelines

### What Makes a Good Project Photo:

✅ **DO:**
- Use natural lighting when possible
- Show the full installation
- Include context (building, interior space)
- Take multiple angles
- Capture details and craftsmanship
- Show scale (people, furniture for reference)
- Use high-resolution cameras/phones

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use blurry or low-resolution photos
- Include clutter or construction debris
- Show incomplete work
- Use heavily filtered images
- Include competitor branding
- Use photos with poor lighting

### Recommended Shots for Each Project:

1. **Wide Shot** - Full view of installation
2. **Detail Shot** - Close-up of craftsmanship
3. **Context Shot** - How it fits in the space
4. **Angle Shot** - Different perspective
5. **Feature Shot** - Highlight special features

## 🎨 Design Tips for Project Photos

### Composition:
- Rule of thirds
- Show scale and context
- Include people if possible (for scale)
- Capture reflections and light effects

### Timing:
- Golden hour (early morning/late afternoon)
- Avoid harsh midday sun
- Night shots for lit installations
- Different seasons/weather for variety

### Post-Processing:
- Basic brightness/contrast adjustment OK
- Keep colors natural
- Don't over-edit or filter
- Maintain professional appearance
